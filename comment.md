**1. **2. Modular sector exclusions (single prime $p=268435399$, not exact proofs).** Restricting to $Y$-even $G$-semi-invariant $D$, parametrizing the exact first equation, reducing the second equation modulo $F$ to quadrics in the free parameters, and dehomogenizing by random linear forms vanishing on $F\cdot S_{d-6}$ (so $D\in(F)$ is excluded by construction), msolve Gröbner bases mod $p$ are the unit ideal for **every $Y$-even character block at degrees 8 and 9** (95 and 108 $E_1$-admissible blocks; degrees 6 and 7 reproduce the known exclusions and the Fermat control recovers the joins). The same holds for the $X_2\leftrightarrow Y_2$-symmetric sectors through degree 12 ($\varepsilon=-1$) and degree 9 ($\varepsilon=+1$); at degree 10 the $\varepsilon=+1$ blocks with about 63 free parameters exceeded 14 GB and are inconclusive. Two restricted families were also empty mod $p$: $N=A\,g$ with $D=g(F)$ for all semi-invariant $g$ through degree 11, and $N=A\,g+K$ ($K$ Koszul, complete for $D\in J(F)$) through degree 9. Finally 6,200 binomial pencils $D=m_1^k+b\,m_2^k$ (orbit products of $m_1-cm_2$, $\deg m_i\le 6$, all $b\in\overline{\mathbb F}_p$ by projected-determinant interpolation) have no solution through degree 12. A modular unit ideal or modular inconsistency is strong evidence but not a characteristic-zero proof; no claim beyond that is made.

**3.
